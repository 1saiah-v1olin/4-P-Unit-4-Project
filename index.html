Fountains of Rome - Ottorino Resphigi

By Isaiah Brown

Fountains of Rome is the first piece of Ottorino Resphigi's Roman Trilogy. Like Pines of Rome, Fountains captures the essence of the city in the lens of various fountains across Rome. Resphigi uses various instruments to create a vivid portrayal of the city's activity throughout the day. He incorporates ancient mythology and natural sounds to bring the fountains to life.


  • The first movement is The Fountain of Valle Giulia at Dawn, depicting a pastoral landscape as the sun rises. Cattle walk by in the morning mist near the Valle Giulia.
  • The second movement is The Triton Fountain in the Morning. The fountain watches as freshwater spirits known as naiads dance as Triton, the sea demigod, blows his conch shell in the morning sun.
  • The third movement is The Trevi Fountain at Noon. The fountain stands as the centerpiece of the square, as Neptune is carried across the waters in a chariot drawn by sea horses.
  • The fourth movement is The Villa Medici Fountain at Sunset. All the activity has calmed for the day, and the fountain sits as the sun sets for the evening.
